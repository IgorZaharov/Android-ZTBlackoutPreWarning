* Application Developer is third-party not connected to Zhytomyr Oblast Electrical Grid Operator (ZhytomyrOblEnergo) and not responsible for validity of information on website, possible power outages outside of planned schedule or emergency outages.
* User preferences (selected queue, notification timing, theme, language, and mini-game scores) are stored strictly locally on the user's device (SharedPreferences).
* No personal data, accounts, location, or analytics are collected or transmitted to third-party servers.
